About HES

A hybrid U.S.–India team built for construction speed

Hiregoudar Engineering Services is headquartered in San Francisco, California, with execution teams in Bengaluru, India. We serve 20+ nationwide clients with comprehensive solutions spanning engineering, design, management, documentation, and on-site staffing.

Our hybrid operational model emphasizes faster delivery, reduced overhead, and increased profitability for architects, developers, general contractors, and subcontractors.
